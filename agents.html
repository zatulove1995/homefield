Questions agents ask

Straight answers.

Will this burn my email domain? +
No — that's the whole point. Homefield sends every campaign from managed, warmed, isolated infrastructure that we own and monitor. Your real business domain, the one your clients and closings run through, is never used for cold volume.
What exactly does Scout do? +
Scout watches your MLS. Every time you list or close a home, it automatically circle-prospects the nearest 250–1,000 homeowners and sends them a personalized just-listed / just-sold sequence from our infrastructure — then qualifies the replies and books appointments for you.
Are the homeowners exclusive to me? +
Yes. We lock your area and dedupe across the whole network, so the homeowners you reach aren't getting the same campaign from other Homefield agents. No shared leads, no racing three other agents to the phone.
Where does the homeowner data come from? +
Parcel and ownership records matched to verified emails (and phone where available), continuously refreshed and suppression-scrubbed. Around 3.1M homeowners are matched and reachable across the network today.
Is cold-emailing homeowners compliant? +
Homefield operates under federal cold-email rules (CAN-SPAM) plus state requirements, with unsubscribe handling and auto-suppression built into every send. Compliance is managed for you rather than left as your problem.
Do I need any tech setup? +
No. You connect your MLS and we handle the data, infrastructure, warmup, and sending. Most agents are live with their first campaign in about 48 hours.
