Pricing questions

Straight answers.

Which product should I start with? +
Start with Neighborhood. It's the main product and it runs off work you're already doing, so it starts producing without you changing anything. Add Prospect once you want net-new customers beyond the homes around your jobs.
Do I pay per lead? +
No. Both products are flat monthly. You're not bidding against other businesses for the same homeowner, and a good month doesn't cost you more than a slow one.
Is there a contract? +
Neighborhood is month to month — cancel anytime. Prospect asks for an initial engagement long enough to build infrastructure, warm domains, and let campaigns produce, then continues month to month.
What counts as "an event" on Neighborhood? +
Any job, closing, or policy you complete at a home. Each one triggers an email campaign to the surrounding homeowners — up to 250 on Starter, up to 1,000 on Pro.
How fast does it start working? +
Neighborhood is typically live within about 48 hours of connecting your software. Prospect takes roughly two weeks to warm dedicated infrastructure before campaigns run at full volume, with replies usually landing within days of launch.
