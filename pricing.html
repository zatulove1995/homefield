Pricing questions

Straight answers.

Which product should I start with? +
Start with Neighborhood. It's the main product and it runs off work you're already doing, so it starts producing without you changing anything. Add Prospect once you want net-new customers beyond the homes around your jobs.
Do I pay per lead? +
No. You pay a flat monthly rate for a set number of homes reached, so you always know your cost up front and you're never bidding against another business for the same homeowner.
What if a big job comes in after I've used most of my credits? +
Your jobs always fire — we never block a campaign. Two things protect you. First, the prospecting sweep holds back a reserve based on your typical job volume, so it can't eat the credits your work needs. Second, if a busy month runs you past your plan anyway, the campaign still goes out and the extra homes are simply billed at $0.20 each. You'll never miss the moment right after a job because of a credit balance, and you'll never pay overage that our prospecting caused.
Is there a contract? +
No term. Every plan is month to month and you can cancel anytime. We ask for about two weeks up front to warm your sending infrastructure before campaigns run at full volume.
What counts as "an event" on Neighborhood? +
Any job, closing, or policy you complete at a home. Each one fires a campaign to the surrounding homeowners and draws from your monthly pool. You choose the radius; a typical campaign reaches 250–1,000 homes.
How fast does it start working? +
Campaigns are typically live within about 48 hours of connecting your software, running at reduced volume while we warm your sending infrastructure. Full volume takes roughly two weeks, with replies usually landing within days of launch.
