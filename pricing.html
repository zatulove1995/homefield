Pricing questions

Straight answers.

Which product should I start with? +
Start with Neighborhood. It's the main product and it runs off work you're already doing, so it starts producing without you changing anything. Add Prospect once you want net-new customers beyond the homes around your jobs.
Do I pay per lead? +
No. Neighborhood is a flat fee per campaign, so you know the cost before it sends and you're never bidding against another business for the same homeowner. Prospect is a flat monthly retainer.
Is there a contract? +
Neighborhood has no subscription and no term at all — you're only billed when a job fires a campaign, so you can stop any time by doing nothing. Prospect asks for an initial engagement long enough to build infrastructure and warm domains, then continues month to month.
What counts as "an event" on Neighborhood? +
Any job, closing, or policy you complete at a home. Each one fires a single campaign to the surrounding homeowners — 250 homes on Starter, 500 on Growth, 1,000 on Pro — and that campaign is what you're billed for.
How fast does it start working? +
Neighborhood is typically live within about 48 hours of connecting your software. Prospect takes roughly two weeks to warm dedicated infrastructure before campaigns run at full volume, with replies usually landing within days of launch.
