Questions agents ask

Straight answers.

Why can't I just do this myself with a cold email tool? +
You can — and plenty of agents try. The three things that break it are deliverability (cold volume from your own domain gets your real email flagged), data (matching parcels to verified, scrubbed homeowner emails), and follow-through (nobody has time to work every reply between showings). Homefield owns all three, which is why it keeps working after month one.
How is this different from just-sold postcards? +
Postcards cost roughly double per drop, take one to two weeks to land, can't be followed up without paying full price again, and give the homeowner no way to respond except calling you. Homefield fires the same day your deal closes, includes the follow-up sequence, and the homeowner can reply in one tap — which we then qualify and book.
Will this burn my email domain? +
No. Every campaign runs on dedicated domains we buy, warm, and monitor. Your real business domain — the one your clients, lenders, and TCs run through — is never used for cold volume.
How does exclusivity actually work? +
We lock your area, so no other agent on Homefield can run campaigns to those homeowners. We also dedupe across the whole network, so a homeowner never receives two Homefield campaigns from competing agents.
Is cold-emailing homeowners compliant? +
We operate under federal cold-email rules (CAN-SPAM) plus applicable state requirements, with unsubscribe handling and auto-suppression built into every send. Compliance is managed for you rather than left as your problem.
