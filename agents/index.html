Questions agents ask

Straight answers.

Will this burn my email domain? +
No — that's the whole point. We send every email from warmed domains we own and monitor, never your business inbox. The address your clients, lenders, and transaction coordinators run through is never used for cold volume.
Do I need my own listing to run a campaign? +
Campaigns fire off your own MLS activity — anything you list, put under contract, or close. If you want to reach homeowners beyond your own deals, that's what the Prospect add-on is for.
Where do the email addresses come from? +
Parcel and ownership records matched to verified homeowner email addresses, continuously refreshed and suppression-scrubbed. Around 3.1M homeowners are matched and reachable across the network today.
Are the neighbors exclusive to me? +
Yes. We lock your area and dedupe across the whole network, so the homeowners you email aren't getting the same campaign from another agent on Homefield. No shared leads, no racing three other agents to the phone.
What do I actually have to do? +
Connect your MLS once — then nothing. We detect every listing and closing, send the emails, handle the replies, and put booked appointments on your calendar. Most agents are live in about 48 hours.
