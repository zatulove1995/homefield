Pricing questions

Straight answers.

Which product should I start with? +
Start with Neighborhood. It's the main product and it runs off deals you're already doing, so it starts producing without you changing anything. Add Prospect once you want sellers beyond the homes around your own listings.
Do I pay per lead? +
No. Neighborhood is a flat fee per campaign, so you know the cost before it sends and you're never bidding against another agent for the same homeowner. Prospect is a flat monthly retainer.
Is there a contract? +
Neighborhood has no subscription and no term at all — you're only billed when a listing or closing fires a campaign, so you can stop any time by doing nothing. Prospect asks for an initial engagement long enough to build infrastructure and warm domains, then continues month to month.
What counts as a campaign? +
Any home you list, put under contract, or close. Each one fires a single campaign to the surrounding homeowners — 250 homes on Starter, 500 on Growth, 1,000 on Pro — and that campaign is what you're billed for.
What if I close several deals in one month? +
Each one fires its own campaign and is billed separately. If you're consistently running several a month, talk to us — that volume usually prices better on a custom plan or on Prospect.
How fast does it start working? +
Neighborhood is typically live within about 48 hours of connecting your MLS. Prospect takes roughly two weeks to warm dedicated infrastructure before campaigns run at full volume, with replies usually landing within days of launch.
