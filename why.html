Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Why can't I just do this myself with a cold email tool? +
You can — and plenty of people try. The three things that break it are deliverability (cold volume from your own domain gets your real email flagged), data (matching parcels to verified, scrubbed homeowner emails), and follow-through (nobody has time to work every reply). Homefield owns all three, which is why it keeps working after month one.
Will this burn my email domain? +
No. Every campaign runs on dedicated domains we buy, warm, and monitor. Your real business domain — the one your quotes, invoices, and customers run through — is never used for cold volume.
How does exclusivity actually work? +
We lock your area by industry, so no other business in your trade on Homefield can run campaigns to those homeowners. We also dedupe across the whole network, so a homeowner never receives two Homefield campaigns from competing businesses.
Is cold-emailing homeowners compliant? +
We operate under federal cold-email rules (CAN-SPAM) plus applicable state requirements, with unsubscribe handling and auto-suppression built into every send. Compliance is managed for you rather than left as your problem.
Where does the homeowner data come from? +
Parcel and ownership records matched to verified emails (and phone where available), continuously refreshed and suppression-scrubbed. Around 3.1M homeowners are matched and reachable across the network today.
