Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Will this burn my email domain? +
No — that's the whole point. Homefield sends every campaign from managed, warmed, isolated infrastructure that we own and monitor. Your real business domain, the one your quotes, invoices, and customers run through, is never used for cold volume.
What exactly does Scout do? +
Scout watches your schedule. Every time you finish a job at a house, it automatically reaches the surrounding 50–500 homes with a personalized "we were just next door" sequence from our infrastructure — then qualifies the replies and books estimates for you.
Which trades is this for? +
Any home services business that works house by house — roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, pest control, solar, painting, remodeling, cleaning, pool service, and more. If serving one home is a reason for the neighbors to hear from you, Scout fits.
Are the neighbors exclusive to me? +
Yes. We lock your service area by trade and dedupe across the whole network, so the homeowners you reach aren't getting the same campaign from other pros in your trade on Homefield. No shared leads, no racing three other companies to the phone.
Where does the homeowner data come from? +
Parcel and ownership records matched to verified emails (and phone where available), continuously refreshed and suppression-scrubbed. Around 3.1M homeowners are matched and reachable across the network today.
Do I need any tech setup? +
No. You connect your scheduling software and we handle the data, infrastructure, warmup, and sending. Most businesses are live with their first campaign in about 48 hours.
