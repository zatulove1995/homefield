Questions operators ask

Straight answers.

What's the difference between Prospect and Neighborhood? +
Neighborhood is software from $400/mo — it fires automatically off work you already do, reaching the neighbors of every job, closing, or policy. Prospect is a done-for-you service from $3,000/mo — our team builds and runs a full outbound engine to win net-new customers across your entire market. Neighborhood works your backyard; Prospect goes and gets everyone else. Many operators run both.
Will this burn my email domain? +
No. We buy and warm dedicated sending domains for your campaigns and monitor them continuously. Your real business domain is never used for cold volume — that's the single biggest reason operators come to us instead of running it in-house.
How fast do appointments start? +
Infrastructure warmup takes about two weeks before campaigns go live at volume. Replies typically start landing within days of launch, and the pipeline builds from there as we test offers and scale sending.
Do you work with my competitors? +
No. We run one business per industry per market. When you're in, your market is off the table for anyone else running the same play — otherwise we'd be competing against ourselves in the same inboxes.
Is cold-emailing homeowners compliant? +
We operate under federal cold-email rules (CAN-SPAM) plus applicable state requirements, with unsubscribe handling and auto-suppression built into every send. Compliance is managed by us rather than left as your problem.
Is there a contract? +
We ask for an initial engagement long enough to build infrastructure, warm domains, and let campaigns produce — typically 90 days. After that it's month to month.
