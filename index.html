Questions operators ask

Straight answers.

What's the difference between Neighborhood and Prospect? +
Neighborhood is software from $400/mo — it fires automatically off work you already do, reaching the neighbors of every job, closing, or policy. Prospect is a done-for-you service from $3,000/mo — our team builds and runs a full outbound engine to win net-new customers across your entire market. Neighborhood works your backyard; Prospect goes and gets everyone else. Many operators run both.
I'm an insurance agency — how is this different from EverQuote or QuoteWizard? +
Those vendors sell the same lead to three to five agencies, so you're racing competitors to the phone on a form fill the homeowner barely remembers submitting. Homefield reaches homeowners 30–45 days before their renewal, in your agency's name, and what you get is a reply asking about coverage — booked onto your calendar. Your ZIPs are locked to you and nothing is ever resold.
Will this burn my email domain? +
No — that's the whole point. Homefield sends every campaign from managed, warmed, isolated infrastructure that we own and monitor. Your real business domain, the one your quotes, invoices, and customers run through, is never used for cold volume.
Which industries is this for? +
Real estate agents (triggered by listings and closings), home services businesses (triggered by completed jobs — roofing, HVAC, plumbing, solar, landscaping, pest, remodeling and more), and home insurance agencies (triggered by new policies and storm activity, or targeted by renewal window).
Are the homeowners exclusive to me? +
Yes. We lock your area by industry and dedupe across the whole network, so the homeowners you reach aren't getting the same campaign from a competitor on Homefield. No shared leads, no racing someone else to the phone.
How fast does it start working? +
Neighborhood is typically live within about 48 hours of connecting your software. Prospect takes roughly two weeks to warm dedicated infrastructure before campaigns run at full volume, with replies usually landing within days of launch.
Is cold-emailing homeowners compliant? +
We operate under federal cold-email rules (CAN-SPAM) plus applicable state requirements, with unsubscribe handling and auto-suppression built into every send. Compliance is managed for you rather than left as your problem.
