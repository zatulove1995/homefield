Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Will this burn my email domain? +
No — that's the whole point. We send every email from warmed domains we own and monitor, never your business inbox. The address your quotes, invoices, and customers run through is never used for cold volume.
Where do the email addresses come from? +
Parcel and ownership records matched to verified homeowner email addresses, continuously refreshed and suppression-scrubbed. Around 3.1M homeowners are matched and reachable across the network today.
Are the neighbors exclusive to me? +
Yes. We lock your area by industry and dedupe across the whole network, so the homeowners you email aren't getting the same thing from a competitor on Homefield. No shared leads, no racing someone else to the phone.
What do I actually have to do? +
Connect your job software, MLS, or agency system once — then nothing. We detect the work you complete, send the emails, handle the replies, and put booked appointments on your calendar. Most businesses are live in about 48 hours.
