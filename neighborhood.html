Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Will this burn my email domain? +
No — that's the whole point. Homefield sends every campaign from managed, warmed, isolated infrastructure that we own and monitor. Your real business domain, the one your quotes, invoices, and customers run through, is never used for cold volume.
What's the difference between Neighborhood and Prospect? +
Neighborhood is triggered by work you already do — every job, closing, or policy fires outreach to the surrounding homes. It's a monthly software subscription starting at $400/mo. Prospect is done-for-you cold outbound to win net-new customers across your whole market — fully managed by our team, from $3,000/mo. Many operators run both.
Which industries is this for? +
Real estate agents (triggered by listings and closings), home services businesses (triggered by completed jobs — roofing, HVAC, plumbing, solar, landscaping, pest, remodeling and more), and home insurance providers (triggered by new policies and storm activity).
Are the neighbors exclusive to me? +
Yes. We lock your area by industry and dedupe across the whole network, so the homeowners you reach aren't getting the same campaign from a competitor on Homefield. No shared leads, no racing someone else to the phone.
Where does the homeowner data come from? +
Parcel and ownership records matched to verified emails (and phone where available), continuously refreshed and suppression-scrubbed. Around 3.1M homeowners are matched and reachable across the network today.
Do I need any tech setup? +
No. You connect your job software, MLS, or agency system and we handle the data, infrastructure, warmup, and sending. Most businesses are live with their first campaign in about 48 hours.
